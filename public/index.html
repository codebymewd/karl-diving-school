About Karl Phelps Driving School

We train new and returning drivers to be safe, lawful and confident behind the wheel. Our programme combines classroom theory with supervised road practice, so you learn the rules and the habits that keep you and others safe.

  • Defensive driving at the core of every course
  • Manual and automatic road training
  • Preparation for the FRSC computer-based test
  • Recorded lessons to catch up on anything you miss

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay when I register?

No. Registration creates your account and Student ID. You only pay when you choose a package and enroll.

How do I pay online?

Select a package, choose Enroll Online, make a bank transfer to the school account shown, then upload your payment proof. We verify it and activate your enrollment.

I already paid at the school. What do I do?

Register or log in, choose your package, tap I Paid On-Site, enter your Student ID and upload your receipt where required.

How long does verification take?

Your status shows as Payment Verification Pending until the school confirms it. You will be notified once approved or if anything is needed.

What if I miss a session?

Tell us through the portal with a reason. Where available, recorded lessons let you catch up.

Can I choose manual or automatic?

Our training covers both manual and automatic road driving. Your instructor will confirm how they are scheduled for your package.
